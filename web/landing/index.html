About

Blanked is an AI assistant for healthier screen habits. It helps you understand how your phone pulls you in, create adaptive focus plans, and block the apps and websites that get in the way.

The app is the control center for permissions, blocking, routines, reports, Health context, and subscription settings. Blanked AI is the daily assistant that helps you adjust your plan and stay in control.

Blanked is currently available on iPhone. Android is being prepared for beta access. You can download the app here or ask to join the Android beta by email.
