# Blank Founders And Pricing

## Founders

Founders are the users who signed up before Blank was created or publicly launched.

There is only one founder group. It should not be split into tiers such as founding testers, early beta, or premium founders.

The founder benefit exists to thank them for trusting Blank before the product existed. Communication should be simple, grateful, and concrete.

## Founder Benefits

- Priority access when Blank becomes available.
- 24-hour priority access before the public launch.
- First notice before public launch updates.
- Founder-only acknowledgement in communications.
- Preferential access to the first Blank physical device availability.
- 3-day preferred stock reservation window.
- 50% discount on the first Blank Physical Device with code `Founders`.
- Any founder benefit should apply only to users already signed up before public launch.

Do not promise lifetime free access, subscription discounts, or future features that are not implemented.

## Pricing Model

- Blank Physical Device launch price: 12.99 EUR.
- Pricing should increase gradually after the first launch phase.
- Future subscription: 1.99 EUR per month.
- The subscription is not implemented yet and must be described as future/planned only.

## Communication Rule

Founder messaging should lead with gratitude, not scarcity.

Recommended positioning:

"Te apuntaste antes de que Blank existiera. Queremos agradecertelo incluyendote en el Founder Program de Blank: acceso prioritario y 50% de descuento en tu primer Blank Fisico con el codigo `Founders`."

Avoid presenting founders as a test cohort unless the user explicitly asks them to test something.
